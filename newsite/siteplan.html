Nadia LLC — Behavior Support Framework for Child Care & Parents

Site Name

Nadia

This name is short, memorable, and matches the brand name of the organization (Nadia LLC), making it easy for child care teams and parents to recognize and search for.

Optional domain idea: nadiaframework.com (example)

Site Purpose

The Nadia website provides a clear and practical Behavior Support Framework for child care settings and families. It will explain how the framework works, who it is for (early childhood teams and parents), and how to get started. The site will include an overview of the framework, strategies and tools, a simple contact/interest form, and resources that support consistent behavior practices at school and at home.

Scenarios

  • What is the age range of children this framework is designed for?
  • Is this framework also useful for parents at home, or only for child care staff?

Color Scheme

The color palette is calm and nature-based to communicate care, growth, and emotional safety. The site plan document uses these same colors.

Light Green — #D1FAE5

Use: page background, card backgrounds, soft sections.

Olive Green — #556B2F

Use: headings, buttons/accents, borders, links.

Typography

  • Poppins (Headings): used for titles and section headers to look modern and confident.
  • Inter (Body): used for paragraphs and lists for readability and clean UI.

This site plan document already applies these fonts (Poppins for headings, Inter for body).
